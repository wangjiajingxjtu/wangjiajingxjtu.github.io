The [CCF Submission Guide](https://wangjiajingxjtu.github.io/ccf-submission-guide/) is now online. I built it to help researchers find CCF A/B conferences and journals, their research areas, publishers, websites, and typical submission times.

The guide covers **190 conferences and 148 journals** across 10 research areas. Search by name or abbreviation, open a venue’s details, and follow its official sources. The site also supports offline use and data export.

CCF A/B 类会议与期刊投稿检索站已上线。可以搜索会议或期刊名称，查看分类、等级、全称、出版社、网址和常见投稿时间，也可以离线使用。
{: lang="zh-CN"}

The data snapshot is **November 3, 2026**. Confirmed dates, estimates, and entries needing further review are marked separately. Please check the current official call or journal submission instructions before submitting.
